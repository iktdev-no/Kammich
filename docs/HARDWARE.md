# Kammich Hardware

## Enclosure

The Kammich enclosure is designed around an **HP Elite Slice G2 Mini PC** and a **5" display**.

The enclosure is currently a WIP and is intended to be 3D printed.

## Bill of Materials

- 8× 5×3 mm magnets
- 1× HP DC 7.4 × 5.0 mm to USB-C adapter (20 V PD)
- 4× M3 × 10 mm screws
- 4× M3 brass inserts (M3 × 3 × 4.5 mm)
- PLA filament
- 3D printer
- Soldering iron

## Model

The enclosure is provided as a `.3mf` file and can be opened and modified using Bambu Studio.

The holes are designed for a USB-C cable and mini-HDMI connection to the display. If your display uses full-size HDMI, you will need to modify the model accordingly.
